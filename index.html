Write‑up

Rationale (Design Decisions)

I focus on a single region at a time so patterns aren’t drowned out by scale differences across continents. A stacked area chart shows both total and composition; a Top‑N cap keeps the view readable. Brush + zoom supports close reading, while the legend allows isolating components. The %‑change metric normalizes baselines to compare growth/decline across categories; the absolute metric preserves magnitude for policy relevance. Tooltips provide exact values and percent change (details‑on‑demand). I considered small multiples and treemaps, but they either made temporal change harder to read (treemap) or required too much screen space (small multiples) for a single‑page deliverable.

Development Process

Solo project. Approximately 24 hours total: planning 4, data prep 4, coding 13, polish 3. Most time was spent restructuring quarterly data and getting brush‑zoom to play nicely with stacked layouts. I prototyped with absolute values, then added the percent‑change transform and Top‑N ranking. I verified responsiveness at 360–1100px widths and tuned tick density for narrow screens. Potential future work: pin annotations for the 2020 trough, add export buttons, and persist UI state in the URL.
